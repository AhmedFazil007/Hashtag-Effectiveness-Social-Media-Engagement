# Hashtag Effectiveness & Social Media Engagement

**Marketing research · Survey analysis · Excel · SPSS**

Hashtags are everywhere in social media marketing, but adding more of them doesn't necessarily make a post more engaging. I wanted to explore **how people perceive hashtag use and what kinds of content they associate with stronger engagement**.

This research project is based on survey responses from **340 participants**, with analysis presented in the full report.

## The question I explored

**How do hashtags, content format and the tone of a post relate to perceived visibility and engagement on social media?**

The research looks at engagement-related themes such as content relevance, visual appeal and the role of influencers. I used survey analysis to organize responses and examine patterns.

## What stood out

The research discusses stronger engagement perceptions around **visually appealing content, influencer-led posts and emotionally engaging or humorous messaging**. These are survey-based observations, not proof that a particular hashtag caused more likes, shares or sales.

That distinction matters: a survey can tell us what respondents think and report, but testing actual post performance would require platform-level engagement data.

## What I'd recommend to a marketing team

I'd use the findings as a starting point for a small content experiment:

- Test relevant, specific hashtags against a more general hashtag set.
- Compare content formats while keeping the audience and campaign goal as consistent as possible.
- Measure **reach, engagement rate, saves, clicks and conversions** rather than relying only on impressions or respondents' perceptions.

The goal would be to learn what actually works for a particular audience—not to assume one hashtag strategy works everywhere.
